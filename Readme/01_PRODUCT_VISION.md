# VERICARB — Product Vision

## Name and tagline
- **Name:** VERICARB
- **Expansion:** VERIfied CARBon (working interpretation)
- **Tagline:** Every Credit. One Traceable History.
- **Category:** Continuous carbon-integrity and provenance platform
- **Related concept:** CarbonProof — the continuous integrity, reversal-monitoring, and cross-registry conflict-detection idea incorporated into VERICARB.

## Product thesis
Carbon markets need more than tokenized credits and transaction records. Buyers and reviewers need to inspect the evidence behind a credit, detect conflicting claims, and understand whether new information has changed the risk associated with the underlying climate outcome.

VERICARB connects:
1. Credit and project provenance.
2. AI-assisted evidence review.
3. Duplicate and cross-registry conflict checks.
4. Environmental reversal-risk alerts.
5. Human verification and dispute handling.
6. Explainable, dynamic Carbon Integrity Score.
7. Blockchain-backed audit events.
8. Public verification pages and role-specific dashboards.

## Core product promise
Know where a credit came from, what evidence supports it, whether conflicts have been detected, and whether new evidence has changed its risk status.

## Problems addressed
- Duplicate issuance: the same project activity may appear under multiple programs or registries.
- Double use: the same unit may be used, transferred, or claimed more than once.
- Double claiming: multiple entities may claim the same climate outcome.
- Reversal risk: the underlying environmental outcome may be lost after issuance.
- Stale verification: previously valid evidence may no longer reflect current project conditions.
- Opaque provenance: evidence, verifier decisions, and lifecycle events are hard to inspect across disconnected systems.

These are different problems. A unique ID and blockchain log can help with traceability, but they do not independently prove real-world additionality, permanence, or legal recognition.

## Differentiator
A basic registry answers: “What happened to this credit?”
VERICARB additionally asks: “What new evidence has appeared, what risks have been detected, and has a reviewer confirmed a change in its integrity?”

## Product principles
- Evidence before claims.
- Explainable risk signals, not black-box certification.
- Human review for material decisions.
- Append-only history; never silently rewrite past events.
- Privacy by design: raw evidence remains off-chain.
- Clear distinction between demo data, observed data, model output, and verified findings.
- Interoperability through adapters and normalized schemas.
- Marketplace and token economy are optional future modules, not MVP requirements.

## User roles
- Project Developer: creates projects and submits evidence.
- Reviewer/Auditor: reviews evidence and accepts/rejects assessments.
- Registry/Operator: performs authorized lifecycle actions.
- Buyer: inspects credit status, score, and retirement history.
- Administrator: manages roles, data sources, thresholds, and configuration.
- Public Visitor: searches a credit/project and sees non-sensitive provenance.
